# Bazzite Updater

![Bazzite Updater|1703x1137, 50%](/img/bazzite-updater.png)

!!! info "Bazzite Updater is added with the release of Bazzite 44!"

The new **Bazzite Updater**, made by [Robert French](https://github.com/rfrench3), allows update, rollback, and viewing announcements on a Graphical UI, and can be used entirely with a controller!

---

## Usage

### Updating

Simply press the update button. You may also view its progress by pressing the **Toggle Console** button in the **top right corner**.

---

### Viewing Announcements

![Bazzite Updater Announcements|1683x1137, 50%](/img/bazzite-updater-changelogs.png)

Announcements can be viewed by clicking the **Announcements** entry under the hamburger menu in the **top left corner**.

!!! tip "Announcements are often relevant to Bazzite as a whole, and may not be strictly relevant to your specific Bazzite system."

---

### Rollback Updates

![Bazzite Updater Utilities|1751x1163, 50%](/img/bazzite-updater-utilities.png)

Rolling back updates can be done by clicking the **Other Utilities** entry under the hamburger menu in the **top left corner**.

---

### Manage Layered Packages

![Bazzite Updater Layered Packages|1683x1137, 50%](/img/bazzite-updater-layered-packages.png)

If you have any [layered packages](/Installing_and_Managing_Software/rpm-ostree.md), the Other Utilities page will have a section to display them. It also allows you to remove layered packages if they cause problems during updates.

---

### Rebase System

![Bazzite Updater Rebase Helper|1683x1137, 50%](/img/bazzite-updater-rebase-helper.png)

You can use the Rebase Helper page to switch your Image and Update Branch. Read the [Rebase Guide](./rebase_guide.md) for more details.

---

## Adding Bazzite Updater to Steam

![Add Bazzite Updater to Steam|1751x1163, 50%](/img/bazzite-updater-add-to-steam.png)

Navigate to the Other Utilities page and click "Add Bazzite Updater to your Steam library". Alternatively, in [Bazzite Portal](/Installing_and_Managing_Software/Bazzite_Portal.md), Select **Manage Bazzite → Update your system → Add Bazzite Updater to Steam Big Picture**.
